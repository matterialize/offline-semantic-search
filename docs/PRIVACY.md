# Privacy Policy for Offline Semantic Search

Developer: Matthew Bajorek (Matterialize)

Privacy contact: [matthew.bajorek@matterialize.com](mailto:matthew.bajorek@matterialize.com)

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Offline Semantic Search helps you locate relevant sentences and paragraphs on the webpage you choose to search. This policy covers the extension, rather than the separate Matterialize website.

## Information processed on your device

When you open Offline Semantic Search using its toolbar action or keyboard shortcut, it reads accessible rendered text on the current page and processes your search query to rank and highlight passages. Page text may include personal or sensitive information present on that page. Processing stays on your device and serves only the search feature. The extension does not deliberately extract editable form values or passwords, request your browser history, monitor unrelated tabs, or create a persistent browsing-history database.

## Storage and retention

The extension bundles the files needed for local search. Page text, embeddings, queries and result caches remain in temporary memory associated with the open search panel. It does not intentionally save them to extension storage, synchronize them to an account, or persist them as a document index. Closing the panel terminates its search worker and clears page highlights. The browser controls when process memory is physically reclaimed.

An optional, explicitly invoked developer tool can export locally labeled examples to a file on your device. Exported files can contain your query and page text and remain until you delete them; the extension does not upload them. Uninstalling the extension removes its installed files according to your browser's uninstall behavior.

## Transmission and third parties

Offline Semantic Search does not send page text, queries, embeddings or search results to Matterialize or third-party inference services. It has no account system, advertising, analytics or telemetry and does not use cookies for tracking. Search code and model files are included in the extension; the installed production extension does not download executable code or contact an inference server.

Your browser's extension installation/update mechanisms operate separately from the extension's search feature. If you email the developer, the information you send is used to respond to your request; avoid including sensitive webpage text.

## Access and use

Temporary page access is requested when you invoke the extension. You control whether to open it, what to search, whether to export examples, and when to close or uninstall it. Information is not sold, shared for advertising, used for unrelated purposes, or used to determine creditworthiness or lending eligibility. Matterialize's use of information received through Offline Semantic Search adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Changes and contact

We will update this policy when the extension's data practices change and revise the date above.

For privacy questions, contact Matthew Bajorek at [matthew.bajorek@matterialize.com](mailto:matthew.bajorek@matterialize.com).
