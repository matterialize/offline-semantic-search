Delivery & unboxing

Standard delivery usually takes three to five business days after your order leaves our warehouse.

You’ll receive a tracking link by email as soon as your parcel is on its way.

If your lamp arrives damaged, send customer care a photograph of the damage and your order number so we can arrange a free replacement.

The cardboard packaging can be recycled with your household paper collection.

The box includes the lamp, its power adapter, and a printed quick-start guide.

Make yourself comfortable

Press and hold the button on the base to lower the brightness for evening reading.

A single tap turns the light on or off, and the lamp remembers your last brightness setting.

Double-tap the button to switch between a warm amber glow and a cooler daylight tone.

The adjustable arm lets you direct light toward your book without shining it into someone else’s eyes.

Use the included power adapter with a standard indoor wall socket.

A change of plans

You may return your lamp within thirty days of delivery if it isn’t right for your space.

For a printer-free return, show the QR code from your return confirmation on your phone at the parcel drop-off counter; the staff will print the shipping label for you.

If you no longer have the original box, use any sturdy carton and enough padding to protect the lamp.

Your refund will go back to the original payment method within five business days of our receiving the returned item.

Gifts can be exchanged for store credit with the original purchaser’s order number.

Keep it shining

Unplug the lamp before wiping it with a soft, slightly damp cloth.

Avoid abrasive cleaners because they can scratch the matte finish.

The two-year warranty covers manufacturing faults, including a light that stops working during normal use.

Accidental spills and damage caused by dropping the lamp are not covered by the warranty.

Our repair team can replace the LED module, so you do not need to throw away the whole lamp when the light eventually wears out.

From our studio

Our designers print early sketches on recycled paper before making physical prototypes.

The new sandstone finish will return to the collection next spring.

The studio’s evening newsletter features reading lists and interviews with independent makers.
